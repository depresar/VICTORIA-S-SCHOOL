Workbook · A2–C1Sound like a native

AS WELL AS instead of AND, BE LIKELY TO instead of WILL — and you sound like a native speaker.

1690 ₽

Did you know that using AS WELL AS instead of AND, or BE LIKELY TO instead of WILL makes you sound like a native speaker and earns you extra exam points?

It will rain tomorrow (A1) → It IS LIKELY TO rain tomorrow (C1) The shop sells clothes and cosmetics (A1) → The shop sells clothes AS WELL AS cosmetics (C1)

Who it's for

  • A2–C1 students who want to speak fluently and confidently
  • Anyone preparing for the EGE or international exams
  • Teachers looking for quality materials

The workbook includes

  • 30 advanced phrases: linking words, phrasal verbs, set expressions and high-level synonyms
  • Usage examples for every expression
  • Practice tasksto make the phrases part of your active vocabulary
  • Final test to check yourself

Ask a question
I.

What are lessons like?

Lessons follow the communicative approach: you learn vocabulary and grammar and put them straight into speech. We use Oxford and Cambridge coursebooks alongside authentic materials chosen around each student's interests (podcasts, YouTube videos, articles, songs).

Lessons take place on interactive platforms for smooth, engaging work together.

II.

How long until I can actually hold a conversation in English?

You'll start speaking in your very first lesson, even as a complete beginner. With lessons twice a week, one level takes about 5 months.

There's no such thing as “finishing” English — even at a high level you need to keep it up. That's what this section is for: “Self-study”.

III.

What qualifications do your teachers have?

Our teachers are qualified professionals with university degrees, certificates and teaching experience, each selected by Victoria. We're happy to provide documentation on request. Victoria's documents

IV.

What results do your students get?

EGE scores range from 71 to 96. Students pass international exams and get into universities abroad and Russia's top universities. We're proud of them and share their results regularly.

V.

I don't live in Russia. Can I still pay for lessons?

Yes — our students come from all over the world, and several payment options are available.
