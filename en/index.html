Workbook “Sound like a native” (A2–C1)

Did you know that using AS WELL AS instead of AND, or BE LIKELY TO instead of WILL makes you sound like a native speaker and earns you extra exam points?

It will rain tomorrow (A1) → It IS LIKELY TO rain tomorrow (C1) The shop sells clothes and cosmetics (A1) → The shop sells clothes AS WELL AS cosmetics (C1)

The workbook gives you 30 phrases, phrasal verbs and other vocabulary you can learn and use in any context, in writing and speaking alike!

The workbook includes

  • 30 advanced phrases: linking words, phrasal verbs, set expressions and high-level synonyms you can use anywhere
  • Usage examples: every expression comes with examples so you see how to use it in context
  • Practice tasks: each section has exercises to help you practise the phrases and make them part of your active vocabulary
  • Final test: check your knowledge and make sure you've mastered everything

Who it's for

Ask a question
What are lessons like?

Lessons follow the communicative approach: you learn vocabulary and grammar and put them straight into speech. We use Oxford and Cambridge coursebooks alongside authentic materials chosen around each student's interests (podcasts, YouTube videos, articles, songs).

Lessons take place on interactive platforms for smooth, engaging work together.

How long until I can actually hold a conversation in English?

You'll start speaking in your very first lesson, even as a complete beginner. With regular lessons twice a week, moving up one level takes about 5 months.

There's no such thing as “finishing” English, though — even at a high level you need to keep it up and keep learning. That's what our section is for: “Self-study”.

What qualifications do your teachers have?

Our teachers are qualified professionals with university degrees, certificates and teaching experience, each selected by Victoria, the school's founder. We're happy to provide documentation on request.

What results do your students get?

Our exam preparation works: EGE scores range from 71 to 96. Students pass international exams and get into universities abroad as well as Russia's top universities. We're proud of them and share their results regularly.
